Question 6: Arbitrage Mechanism

Market Mechanics
Oral Defense Question (English)
"In a healthy prediction market, if the price of YES is $0.60 and NO is $0.50 for the same event (summing to $1.10), or if they sum to $0.90, what happens in the market? How do arbitrageurs act to bring the combined price back to $1.00?"
Key Evaluation Points (Expected Answer)
  • Sum > $1.00: Arbitrageurs mint 1 pair for $1.00 and sell both for $1.10, locking in a $0.10 risk-free profit while pushing prices down.
  • Sum < $1.00: Arbitrageurs buy both shares for $0.90 and burn/redeem them with the platform for $1.00 cash, pushing prices up.
