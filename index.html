Defense Procedure Schedule

00:00 – 00:05
(5 Mins)
1. Opening & Committee Introduction Committee Convener

The Committee Chair opens the examination session, introduces the committee members, and outlines the defense rules and time limits.

00:05 – 00:25
(20 Mins)
2. Candidate Oral Presentation Candidate

The candidate presents the research highlights, including background, empirical findings (Brier Scores, Lead-Lag, Favorite-Longshot Bias), and policy implications.

00:25 – 00:65
(40 Mins)
3. Oral Examination & Q&A Session Committee & Candidate

Committee members pose deep-dive questions regarding thesis methodology, market mechanics, counterparty risk, and econometric models. The candidate responds to each query.

00:65 – 00:75
(10 Mins)
4. Committee Closed Deliberation Closed Session

The candidate and observers step out of the room. Committee members evaluate thesis quality, presentation performance, and vote on the final grade/verdict.

00:75 – 00:80
(5 Mins)
5. Result Announcement & Revision Review Public Session

The candidate re-enters the room. The Committee Chair officially announces the defense result, pass/fail status, and details necessary thesis revisions.

Committee Responsibilities
  • Evaluate theoretical background and methodological rigor.
  • Verify candidate's independent understanding of empirical findings.
  • Provide constructive feedback for final thesis revisions.
Candidate Key Focus
  • Keep presentation strictly within the 20-minute limit.
  • Address questions directly with concise, evidence-based answers.
  • Take detailed notes of all committee comments for post-defense edits.
